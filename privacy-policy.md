## 1. Who we are

ChargeSafe is an electric vehicle trip planning application published by Wallace Digital, based in Western Australia. This policy explains what information the app collects, why it is collected, and what we do with it.

We are bound by the Australian Privacy Principles under the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth).

## 2. Summary

- ChargeSafe does **not** require you to create an account.
- ChargeSafe uses your device location to find nearby chargers and plan routes.
- ChargeSafe does **not** sell your personal information.
- ChargeSafe does **not** use your data for advertising or advertising profiles.

## 3. Information we collect

**Location data.** With your permission, ChargeSafe accesses your device's precise location while you are using the app. This is used to show nearby chargers, set your trip starting point, and plan routes. Location is used for app functionality only. If you decline location permission, you can still use the app by entering locations manually.

**Feedback you provide.** If you submit feedback, a charger correction, or log a charging event, we collect the content you provide, which may include the charger location and details of your experience. This is used to improve charger data accuracy for all users.

**App diagnostics.** The app collects basic technical telemetry (such as crash reports and feature-usage signals) to help us find and fix problems. This data is used for app functionality and improvement only.

## 4. What we do not collect

- No account registration, name, or password is required.
- No contact lists, photos, or files on your device.
- No advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking.
- No sale or trade of personal information to third parties.

## 5. How information is used

Information collected is used solely to:

- Plan EV trips and display nearby charging infrastructure
- Improve the accuracy of charger data through user corrections and feedback
- Diagnose and fix technical problems in the app

## 6. Third-party services

ChargeSafe uses Google Maps to display maps and routes. Your use of the map is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. Route and charger data is served from Wallace Digital's own infrastructure over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

## 7. Data storage and security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored on infrastructure controlled by Wallace Digital. Feedback and correction data may be retained to maintain the accuracy of the charger database. Diagnostic data is retained only as long as needed for troubleshooting and improvement.

## 8. Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles you may request access to, or correction of, personal information we hold about you. Because ChargeSafe does not use accounts, most data we hold is not linked to your identity. To make a request or ask a question, contact **privacy@wallacedigital.au**.

## 9. Children

ChargeSafe is a driving-related tool and is not directed at children under 16.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change — for example, if in-app advertising is introduced in a future version — this policy will be updated before those changes take effect, and the "Last updated" date revised. The current version will always be available at wallacedigital.au/privacy.
